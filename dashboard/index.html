Board Method & evidence

Recomputing the board from the shipped data…

#ProtocolCategoryTVL 7d %Implied valueBand P25–P75 Rank rangeVerdict

Band = P25–P75 of the row's value across 200 seeded bootstrap refits of the cleaned peer set; rank range = P10–P90 of its rank across the same refits. Verdicts are farm economics netted in dollars at the band low (Farm), the point estimate only (Thin), or not at all (Skip) — definitions and counts on the Method view.
